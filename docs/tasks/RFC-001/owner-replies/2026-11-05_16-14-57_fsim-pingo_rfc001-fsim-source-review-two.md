Copy a complete packet/header+payload into staging before publication, clear completion BEFORE25h can enable its IRQ, keep source immutable until callback and do not resubmit while busy. 3. Two independent owners/completions are essential. TX callback only releases eZ80 staging storage after last FIFO load. native_frame and associated buffers remain frozen until their appropriate VDP phase finishes: native_latest can advance with physics, native_frame is the captured presentation, native_sent selects its sequence. native_service currently enforces PREPARE reply -> execute/drain composition -> RENDER reply -> HUD/C3/orderedGP -> present count. Do not advance a phase or reuse scene/command/work buffers merely on TX callback; do not permit a second native_run before prior matching reply. A receiver acknowledgement can arrive before foreground consumes TX-done, so both flags must be independent. Preserve token/kind/error checks, fail-closed uncertain renderer cleanup and page history.25h changes producer transport, not native RUN scheduling or VSYNC overlap; no FPS multiplier can be inferred from it. Our live render requests are small (pose patch11+44B; RUN6+11B), so measured benefit may primarily be reduced input/physics service gaps rather than much greater draw throughput. Bulk asset loading is a separate case, not gameplay FPS evidence.

4. ISR/register/timer constraints. Existing receive callback donor utilities/pingo-wireframe/hardware/src/packets.src preserves AF/BC/DE/HL/IX/IY, stores matched VDPL replies and ordinary key edges, performs no MOS/libc/VDU work. New TX callback is a DIFFERENT ISR path: do not reuse the packet receiver ABI or its DE payload convention. Use a small assembly flag store/RET, preserving IX/IY per RFC and any used alternate register state; no floating-point or C bridge without explicit ABI qualification. FSIM is ADL24 (controller/gateway.s and AgonDev build), but .assume ADL alone does not prove runtime MBASE=0. RFC selects its callback encoding from current MB without capturing a new bank context, so establish actual entry MB and prohibit bank changes/unload until TX done. Z80 banked callbacks/upper count semantics remain separate owner qualification, not an FSIM requirement to exercise.
